## Rules

- One column, labels above inputs.
- Group related fields with `FieldSet` and `FieldLegend`, not cards.
- Actions at the end: Cancel as `outline`, then the submit button named for the result, such as "Create customer".

## Usage

Use for more than about five fields or for forms with sections. Three or fewer
fields fit in a `dialog`. Settings that save as they change belong in
`settings-page`.

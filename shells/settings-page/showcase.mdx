## Rules

- Settings that apply immediately use a switch, select or toggle group and confirm with a toast.
- A section that needs saving puts its button at the end of that section only.
- Destructive settings go last, in their own section, behind an alert dialog.

## Usage

Use for account, workspace, notification and billing settings. A single form
with one Save button is a `form-page`.

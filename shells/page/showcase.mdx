## Parts

- `Page` sets the width (`narrow`, `default` or `full`) and the page gutters.
- `PageHeader` holds the only `h1`, a one-sentence description, and the actions on the right. Tabs that switch the whole screen go in its children.
- `PageToolbar` holds search, filters and view switches. `end` puts controls on the right.
- `PageBody` stacks the content.
- `PageSection` groups content under an `h2` with optional actions.

## Usage

Reach for an archetype shell first: list, detail, form, settings or overview.
Compose these parts yourself only when none of them fits, and if the result
becomes a pattern you reuse, turn it into a new shell under
`components/shells/` with a justification header (see `design/shells.md`).

## Rules

- `stats`: two to four cards, each one number with a label and, if useful, the change since last period.
- `children`: panels in a two-column grid. Give a panel `data-span="full"` to take the whole row. Charts are full or half width, never thirds.
- Chart colors come only from the `chart-*` tokens.

## Usage

Use for home screens and reporting summaries. Screens for finding and acting on
records are `list-page`, with a small summary if needed.

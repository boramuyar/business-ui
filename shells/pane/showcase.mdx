## Parts

- `Pane`: fills its container as a column. Give the container a height.
- `PaneHeader`: 40px tall. `leading` holds a back or menu button, `actions` holds icon buttons, and the children are the title.
- `PaneTitle`: the heading. Use `asChild` around `DialogTitle` or `SheetTitle` when the pane is a sheet's content.
- `PaneToolbar`: search and filters, with `end` for controls on the right.
- `PaneBody`: the only part that scrolls.
- `PaneFooter`: one or two equal-width buttons, primary last.

## Usage

Use for Office task panes, browser extension popups, side panels and the
content of full-height sheets. Lists inside a pane use `item`; an empty body
uses `empty`. Full-width screens use `page` or one of its archetypes.

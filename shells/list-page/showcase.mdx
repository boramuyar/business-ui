## Slots

- `actions`: one primary "New …" button, plus outline buttons for import or export.
- `filters`: search first, then filter popovers or selects.
- `view`: the right side of the toolbar, for a list or board switch or column settings.
- `summary`: up to four stat cards, only when the numbers change what people do next.
- `children`: a table, or a card grid when records are visual. Show `empty` when there are no rows.

## Usage

Use for customers, invoices, orders and users. Open a record in `detail-page`,
or in a `sheet` when people need the list to stay visible.

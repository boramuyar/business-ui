## Usage

Use `progress` when completion is measurable - uploads, imports, multi-step
flows. For unknown durations use `spinner` or `skeleton`.

## Circle

`ProgressCircle` is the same value as a ring, for a screen given over to one
task. Put a short count in its center and list the steps below it with
`progress-steps`.

<Preview name="progress-circle" />

## Result

When the task ends, `ProgressResult` takes the ring's place at the same size: green with a check when everything
succeeded, amber with an exclamation mark when it finished short of what it aimed for, red with a cross when it
failed or was stopped. Drop the count; the heading below says the outcome.

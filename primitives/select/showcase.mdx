## Glyph

`glyph` on `SelectTrigger` puts an icon or a short symbol in a 28px cell at the start, the same cell a `number-field`
gives its glyph, so in a column of fields and selects every value starts at the same edge. Give the trigger an
`aria-label`, since the glyph doesn't name it, and give the content `checkPosition="start"`: every option starts with
the glyph's cell, the picked one showing its check there, so the list opens over the trigger with its edges on the
trigger's and the picked option on the value.

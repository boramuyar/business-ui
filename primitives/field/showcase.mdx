## Variants

Fields lay out vertically by default; `orientation` supports `horizontal` and
container-query-driven `responsive`.

<Preview name="field-variants" />

## States

`invalid` turns the field destructive and `required` adds an asterisk to the
label. `Field` owns the ids: `FieldLabel`, `FieldDescription` and `FieldError`
connect themselves, and `FieldInput` and `FieldTextarea` connect the control.
For any other control, spread `useFieldControl()` onto it. `FieldError`
renders single or multiple messages.

<Preview name="field-states" />

## Composition

`FieldSet` and `FieldLegend` group related fields; `FieldSeparator` labels the
boundary between groups.

<Preview name="field-composition" />

## Usage

Use `field` as the standard scaffolding for every form control. It replaces
ad-hoc label/description/error markup; pair it with `input`, `textarea`,
`select`, `checkbox`, `radio-group`, and `switch`.

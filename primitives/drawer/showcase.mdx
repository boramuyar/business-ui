## Variants

The drawer opens from the bottom edge by default. The `direction` prop moves it
to a side edge, for touch layouts only.

<Preview name="drawer-variants" tall />

## Composition

Scrollable content stays usable inside the drawer.

<Preview name="drawer-composition" tall />

## Usage

Use `drawer` as the mobile version of a dialog or sheet, switched with
`use-mobile`. On desktop use `sheet` or `dialog`; to confirm a destructive
action use `alert-dialog`.

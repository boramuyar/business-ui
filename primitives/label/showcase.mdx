## Variants

Labels attach to any control through `htmlFor`.

<Preview name="label-variants" />

## States

The label dims itself when its peer control or group is disabled.

<Preview name="label-states" />

## Usage

Always label form controls. Inside structured forms prefer `field` with
`FieldLabel`, which wraps this primitive and adds layout, description, and
error handling.

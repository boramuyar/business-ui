title: Button group

---

## Variants

Horizontal (default) and vertical orientations.

<Preview name="button-group-variants" />

## Composition

`ButtonGroupText` labels a segment, `ButtonGroupSeparator` splits same-variant
buttons, and inputs join the group seamlessly.

<Preview name="button-group-composition" />

A split button pairs a primary action with a `dropdown-menu`.

<Preview name="button-group-split-button" tall />

## Usage

Use `button-group` for related actions such as split buttons, pagination
arrows or zoom controls. To pick one option from a set, use `toggle-group`.

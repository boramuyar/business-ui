## Pane size

`size="pane"` holds a whole view in a narrow host, such as an add-in's task pane. Below `sm` it fills the screen from just under the host's bar to the bottom, while the scrim still covers the bar, so it is clear the dialog sits above the app. From `sm` up it is a large centered dialog. Its content lays itself out: a header with a border and a body that scrolls, such as the pane shell's `PaneHeader` and `PaneBody`. The host sets `--overlay-top-inset` on the root to the height of its bar, or of a menu it paints over the corner, whichever is taller.

## Usage

Use a dialog for short, focused tasks. For destructive confirmations use
`alert-dialog`; for long side content use `sheet` or `drawer`.

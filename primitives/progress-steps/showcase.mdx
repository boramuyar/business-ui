## States

A circle shows the share of steps done; the list names each step. One step
runs at a time.

<Preview name="progress-steps-running" />

A failed step stops the work; the steps after it stay pending.

<Preview name="progress-steps-failed" />

A step that finished without the result it aimed for, such as a solver that missed its goal, is a warning: the
work goes on, and the step says what fell short.

<Preview name="progress-steps-warning" />

## Usage

Use `progress-steps` when people wait on work made of a few named steps.
Only the running step shows a spinner. List the steps the work really goes
through; never invent steps to fill the wait.

## Variants

The sheet opens from `right` (default), `left`, `top`, or `bottom`.

<Preview name="sheet-variants" tall />

## Composition

A settings form inside a sheet with footer actions.

<Preview name="sheet-composition" tall />

## Usage

Use `sheet` for secondary tasks and detail panels that should not lose page
context. For mobile-style swipe-to-dismiss panels use `drawer`; for blocking
tasks use `dialog`.

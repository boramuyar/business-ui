## Variants

Right-click the surface to open the menu - items, checkbox and radio items,
a submenu, shortcuts, and a destructive item.

<Preview tall name="context-menu-variants" />

## Usage

Use `context-menu` for power-user shortcuts on a specific region; always keep
a visible alternative for the same actions, since right-click is not
discoverable. For button-triggered menus use `dropdown-menu`.

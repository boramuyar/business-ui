## Variants

The full pattern: previous/next, numbered links, an active page, and an
ellipsis.

<Preview name="pagination-basic" />

Previous/next accept custom `text` labels - the primitive detail pages use
them for prev/next primitive navigation.

<Preview name="pagination-text" />

## Usage

Use `pagination` for numbered page navigation in tables and lists. In SPA
routes intercept the click and call the router; the links render real
anchors for accessibility.

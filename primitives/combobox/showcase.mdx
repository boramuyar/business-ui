## Composition

Multiple selection renders chips inside the anchor; `ComboboxGroup` and
`ComboboxLabel` organize grouped lists the same way `select` does.

<Preview tall name="combobox-multiple" />

## Usage

Use `combobox` for more than about 15 options or lists people know by name.
For 6 to 15 options use `select`; for fewer than 6 use `radio-group` or
`toggle-group`. For command-style navigation use `command`.

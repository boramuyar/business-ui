## Glyphs

A short symbol or an icon inside the field is its drag handle: drag it sideways to change the value, one step per
2px, ten times bigger with Shift and ten times smaller with Alt. Its full name is the tooltip. The field also takes
shorthand such as `25k` or `1.5m`, a trailing unit, and arithmetic such as `=base+0.5` with the names passed in
`variables`; while typing, the result shows in brand after the text. Enter keeps the typing and Escape drops it.

## Label as handle

`useNumberScrub` gives any element the same drag. Spread it on a `ScrubLabel` and pass `scrubbing` to the field, so
the field's edge shows the drag.

## Usage

Use `number-field` for rates, sizes, limits and counts in a dense settings row. For a few usual values with the odd
custom one use `preset-number-field`; for text use `input`.

## States

Unchecked, checked, indeterminate, disabled, and invalid.

<Preview name="checkbox-states" />

The indeterminate state coordinates a parent with its children.

<Preview name="checkbox-indeterminate" />

## Composition

Inside a `field` row, with or without a description.

<Preview name="checkbox-composition" />

## Usage

Use `checkbox` for independent yes/no choices and multi-select lists. For
mutually exclusive options use `radio-group`; for immediate on/off settings
use `switch`.
